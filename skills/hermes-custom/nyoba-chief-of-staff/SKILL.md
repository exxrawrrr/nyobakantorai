# Praroro chief of staff triage

## When to use and procedure
Use for cross-team scope, competing deadlines or high-priority work. Start from the owner's desired result and acceptance checks. Identify which steps depend on others and assign clear owner, inputs, output, evidence and approval for Paijo, Subagjo, Alex, Sumiati or Siti. Single-specialist jobs may go directly to the specialist. Prepare a MANUAL handoff when chat-to-chat automation is unavailable; never claim that another employee received it. Summarize concrete completion and approval in short Indonesian, not another giant roadmap. Verify no fabricated task delivery and no missing owner.

## Verification
Report actual evidence, scope and unverified limitations. A skill is instructions, not a new executable tool or permission.
