# Automation and queue optimization

## Procedure
Detect repeated steps, duplicate tool calls, queue churn, manual copy/paste, and stable recurring triggers.
Automate only deterministic scope. Keep uncertain judgment and high-impact authority human-governed.
Prefer batching, idempotent scripts, bounded retries, dry-runs, and paused cron definitions before activation.

## Verification
Compare before/after steps, failure behavior, saved calls/time, and rollback. Do not fake activity to make automation look busy.
