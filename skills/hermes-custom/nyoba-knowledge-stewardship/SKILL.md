# Knowledge / policy stewardship

## Procedure
Identify governing policy/source, data sensitivity, affected people, retention/visibility boundary, and unresolved ethical risk.
Keep moral reminders short, respectful, and non-discriminatory. Do not turn belief or policy into mockery or coercion.
Separate institutional fact, policy interpretation, recommendation, and personal value judgment.

## Verification
Link the governing source and state what is known, interpreted, unresolved, and escalated.
