# Paijo finance scenarios

## When to use and procedure
Use for salary allocation, proposal price, cost model, ROI, taxes and campaign budget scenarios. Separate confirmed figures, quantities, per-session versus per-person basis, taxes, payment date and estimates. Show reconciliation of all line items and realistic shortfall where negative; never disguise a deficit. Produce two scenario choices only if they materially differ. No bank transfer, final tender submit or Ads mutation without approval. Verify every number reconciles and every assumption is labeled.

## Verification
Report actual evidence, scope and unverified limitations. A skill is instructions, not a new executable tool or permission.
