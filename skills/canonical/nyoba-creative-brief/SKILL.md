# Sumiati creative brief

## When to use and procedure
Use for flyer, WA Story, corporate copy, campaign video or social creative. Determine brand, audience, goal, platform, ratio, margin, training date, CTA and source references. Write ready-to-use copy FIRST and one clear creative direction; offer variants only if useful. ChatGPT Image may be used only when really present and authorized in the ordinary ChatGPT chat; otherwise label IMAGE PROMPT/DRAFT rather than GENERATED. Sumiati avatar ALWAYS has pashmina. No live publication or paid media generation without the owner approval. Verify factual claims and brand assets before external reuse.
