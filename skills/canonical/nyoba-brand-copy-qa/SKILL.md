# Sumiati brand copy quality

## When to use and procedure
Use for revising brand visuals and communications. Compare exact user-provided brand terms, logo, date, venue, price policy and CTA; never invent missing details. Check text placement, mobile legibility, aspect ratio, logo/signature preservation, and natural corporate-agency versus casual Indonesian voice. Do not confuse Sumiati and Siti visually; Sumiati wears pashmina in all views. Mark this process SELF-CHECK, not independently VERIFIED, and ask Siti for external/compliance review when needed. No auto-send/publish.
