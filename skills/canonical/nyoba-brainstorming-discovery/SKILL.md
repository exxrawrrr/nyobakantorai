# Brainstorming & Discovery

Separate **problem framing** from solution enthusiasm.

1. Restate the desired outcome and user/job-to-be-done.
2. Extract hard constraints, authority limits, evidence needs, and non-goals.
3. Generate at least 2 materially different approaches when choice is real.
4. Compare trade-offs: value, risk, reversibility, cost, complexity, measurement.
5. Surface assumptions and the fastest test that would invalidate each.
6. Converge on one design and define acceptance criteria before execution.

For creative work, diverge broadly first, then narrow by audience, proof, brand, channel, and objective. Novelty never overrides factual integrity.
