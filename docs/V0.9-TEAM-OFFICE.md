# v0.9.0 Team Office — CHAT 29

Status: **IMPLEMENTED AT REPOSITORY CONTRACT LEVEL / PUBLICATION BLOCKED**

CHAT 29 adds governed human collaboration while preserving the existing AI workforce, Connector Center, Approval Center, Project Brain, Artifact Workspace, Cost Governor, and evidence boundaries.

## What changed

- human identities are separate from employee identities;
- workspaces contain exact project memberships;
- role permissions are policy-owned and content-addressed;
- RBAC decisions are derived from canonical identity + membership + role;
- approval delegation is bounded, exact-scope, time-limited, and cannot impersonate the legacy owner;
- connectors require both a valid Connector Center route and exact workspace/project binding;
- workspace/project budget envelopes sit above existing Mission/provider cost governance;
- Project Brain visible project/shared scopes are derived from active memberships;
- Artifact/Evidence access requires exact shared-resource binding;
- shared audit history is append-only and hash chained.
