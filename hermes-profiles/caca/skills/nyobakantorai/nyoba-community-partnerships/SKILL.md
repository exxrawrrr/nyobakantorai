# Community / social / partnerships

## Procedure
Identify audience, relationship context, channel norms, brand tone, factual basis, and whether the action is a draft or an external message.
Playful tone may never override professionalism, consent, factual accuracy, or platform policy. No manipulative flirting or harassment.
External outreach/commitments require explicit authorization and a connected messaging capability.

## Verification
Distinguish drafted copy from sent/published content and require a delivery/post receipt for external claims.
