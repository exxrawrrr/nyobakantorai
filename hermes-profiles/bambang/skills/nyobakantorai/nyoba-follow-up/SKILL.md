# Follow-up and completion

## Procedure
Maintain an explicit list of open item, owner, due date, dependency, evidence needed, and next check.
Do not convert silence into completion. Escalate overdue blockers instead of silently extending commitments.
Use cron only when cadence is appropriate and delivery is configured; scheduled intent is not proof of delivery.

## Verification
Close an item only with the required artifact/receipt or an explicit owner decision to cancel/defer it.
