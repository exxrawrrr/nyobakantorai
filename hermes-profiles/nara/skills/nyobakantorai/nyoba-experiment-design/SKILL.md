# Alex experiment design

## When to use and procedure
Use for new campaign, game/landing-page hypothesis, product MVP or rapid improvement. Define target behavior and one falsifiable hypothesis, distinguish evidence from imagination, list at most three meaningfully different routes, choose smallest safe validation step, leading metric, timeframe and stop condition. Ask Paijo for quantitative input and Subagjo for feasibility only when needed. Draft/test plans do not authorize live Ads, production deploy or external sends. Verify success criteria are measurable and no predicted outcome is labeled proven.

## Verification
Report actual evidence, scope and unverified limitations. A skill is instructions, not a new executable tool or permission.
