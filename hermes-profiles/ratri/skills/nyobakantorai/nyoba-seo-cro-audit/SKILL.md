# SEO / CRO audit

## Procedure
1. Identify search intent and target page/funnel.
2. Inspect crawl/indexability signals, content relevance, internal links, metadata/schema basics, performance, and conversion friction.
3. Separate observed page facts from Search Console/GA4 concepts unless live data is actually connected.
4. Prioritize by expected impact, confidence, effort, and measurement plan.

## Verification
Cite the inspected URL/artifact and the specific evidence for every material finding. Do not claim ranking or conversion improvement before measurement.
