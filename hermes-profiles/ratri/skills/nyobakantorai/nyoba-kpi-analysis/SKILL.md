# Paijo KPI analysis

## When to use and procedure
Use for Ads, GSC, GA4, SEO, lead funnel, CPC/CPA/CVR/CTR/ROAS. Identify source as genuine LIVE CONNECTOR, OWNER-PROVIDED or ILLUSTRATIVE and show timeframe, metric definition, units and denominators. Recalculate with traceable formulas, identify duplicate conversions and attribution differences, show meaningful uncertainty. Give key numeric answer before lengthy interpretation. Do not claim access to Ads, CRM or accounting from a skill alone; no budget/campaign writes without the owner's exact approval. Verify the reader can reproduce every material number.

## Verification
Report actual evidence, scope and unverified limitations. A skill is instructions, not a new executable tool or permission.
